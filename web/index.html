    Commands

    / Ctrl/Cmd+Kcommand palette: add a module, insert an assembly, open a patch, any action
    Ctrl/Cmd+Ssave the patch to Your patches
    Ctrl/Cmd+Bcollapse / expand the sidebar
    ?this list
    Esccancel a connection, finish choosing or arranging what a card shows, close a dialog

    Selection

    click / Shift-click a titleselect a card / add it to the selection; drag on the empty canvas to select an area
    Ctrl/Cmd+Aselect every card on the canvas
    Ctrl/Cmd+Dduplicate the selection with its settings and the cables among it
    Ctrl/Cmd+Gmake an assembly from the selection
    Delete Backspaceremove the selection
    Bbypass / un-bypass the selection
    ←→↑↓nudge the selection (with Shift: ten times further)
    Ctrl/Cmd+Z Shift+Ctrl/Cmd+Zundo, redo
    Ctrl/Cmd + wheelzoom the canvas (pinch on a touch screen)
    right-click a titlethe card’s ⋯ menu

    Patching

    click a jack, then anotherconnect (dragging works too)
    click a cabledisconnect it; grey routes are built into the module
    drop on a thumbmodulate that parameter (depth in its ⋮ menu); Alt-drag a thumb to patch from it
    Shift-click a jackshow it on the scope
    Ctrl/Cmd-click a rowin an expanded assembly: show it on the collapsed card, or take it off

    Controls

    drag a sliderset its value; press anywhere on the bar to jump there
    Shift-dragfine: a slider at a tenth of the speed, a knob or envelope corner finer too
    double-clicka knob, wheel or envelope display: back to its default; a heading or assembly title: rename it
