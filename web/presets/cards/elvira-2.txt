# Keys F3..B3 and C4..F4 each trigger one of the four voices at its own tune; the
# tunes below are intervals, so a preset is a chord. "mod" is the crush rate (1 = clean).
# Every preset is silent until a key or PLAY (#1086): a drone is held notes on the PATTERN
# track (presets/patterns/defaults.json), so PLAY starts it and STOP lets it go; drone stays 0.
# CUTOFF and the LFOs' FREQ are 0..1 (#858): cutoff 20 Hz x 800^value, freq 0.02 Hz x 2^(15.6 value).
# A sequence step is in octaves (0.25 = 3 st, 0.5 = 6 st); step 1 stays 0 so a stopped
# transport leaves the chord as tuned.
