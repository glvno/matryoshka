# Factory sound roles

These briefs explain the intended use of the scoped factory banks. They are listening prompts, not aesthetic approval or claims of exact sonic parity. See [Making factory presets](PRESET-AUTHORING.md) for the repeatable authoring and review workflow.

## Io 4

Four-voice oscillator/filter character, pulse-width movement and ensemble. The two Compu sounds study useful roles from Roland’s original preset list; they do not copy undocumented factory settings. Keep the pad, pluck and modulated sounds because their envelopes and motion serve different arrangements. [Reference](https://support.roland.com/hc/en-us/articles/201955089-JUPITER-4-Technical-Specifications).

| Preset | Intended use |
|---|---|
| Init | Open subtractive starting point for chords and control exploration. |
| Ensemble Pad | Slow, wide chord bed with ensemble movement. |
| Arp Pluck | Short articulated figure that leaves room between notes. |
| Unison Lead | Dense foreground melody with a firmer attack. |
| S&H Filter | Stepped filter motion for a rhythmic electronic texture. |
| PWM Brass | Pulse-width brass chords with a moving body. |
| Compu Piano | Short chord comping inspired by the original Piano role; a modern study, not an electric-piano model. |
| Compu Bass | Compact low-register sequence inspired by the original Bass role; a role study, not a factory-patch transcription. |

## Monolith D

The mixer, ladder filter and separate contours support substantial basses and expressive monophonic leads. Rhea Plucked Bass follows an original Moog chart’s oscillator and envelope recipe. Its cutoff conversion is approximate. Soft Flute remains a separate two-oscillator interpretation; it is not the original chart’s noisy flute. [Reference](https://medias.audiofanzine.com/files/minimoog-sounds-by-tom-rhea-ld-480638.pdf).

| Preset | Intended use |
|---|---|
| Init | General three-oscillator lead and bass starting point. |
| Fat Bass | Dense low-register foundation from two low saws and a square. |
| Glide Lead | Sustained melody with sliding pitch and an open resonant filter. |
| Square Lead | Hollow pulse lead that separates from a saw-heavy arrangement. |
| Pink Wind | Noise-only swell for transitions and environmental texture. |
| Soft Flute | Gentle triangle-based legato line with a soft onset. |
| Muted Horn | Rounded brass-like attack and steady body for middle-register melody. |
| Rhea Plucked Bass | Single-oscillator plucked low line from Moog Sound Charts, printed page 6; leaner than Fat Bass. |

## Sallen 20

The paired high-pass/low-pass filters, ring modulation and external-signal controls define this bank. Keep the aggressive sounds as deliberate contrast, and add a restrained reed for ordinary melodic work. The MS-20 manual is the control reference; Soft Reed is an authored study, not a named factory-patch recreation. [Reference](https://www.korg.com/us/support/download/product/1/153/).

| Preset | Intended use |
|---|---|
| Init | General dual-filter synth voice. |
| Acid Scream | Resonant, biting lead for short phrases; final Output reduced while internal drive remains. |
| Growl Bass | Low, rough bass with a substantial body. |
| Ring Mod Bell | Inharmonic struck accents and tuned percussion. |
| Noise Sweep | Broadband transition and percussion wash. |
| ESP Warble | Unstable pitch/filter motion for electronic effects. |
| Soft Reed | Filtered square-wave melody with less high-frequency bite. |

## Silver Box

The single oscillator, resonant filter, accent and slide are the useful constraints. These are authored acid-bass directions, not claims of a fixed original factory bank. Use short and sustained articulation to distinguish the envelope choices. [Reference](https://www.roland.com/global/products/rc_tb-303/).

| Preset | Intended use |
|---|---|
| Init | Basic sequenced bass for hearing the core controls. |
| Acid Squelch | Resonant acid phrase with an expressive moving top. |
| Rubber Square | Hollow, rounded square-wave bass. |
| Deep Sub 303 | Low foundation with a restrained upper spectrum. |
| Short Zap | Very short synthetic punctuation. |
| Long Sweep | Long filter travel for sparse phrases and transitions. |

## Vernon 100

Dual-voice interaction and cross-modulation matter more than covering generic piano or string roles. Preserve the contrasting stepped, ringing and slow sounds. The original EML documentation is a historical control reference; no recorded original-instrument match is asserted. [Reference](http://www.synthfool.com/docs/EML/100_series/).

| Preset | Intended use |
|---|---|
| Init | Two-oscillator exploratory starting point. |
| Fat Duo Lead | Thick foreground line from paired oscillators. |
| Wobble Bass | Low bass with audible periodic movement. |
| Stepped Pulse | Discrete modulation for mechanical rhythmic phrases. |
| Metal Clang | Bright inharmonic struck accents; intentional high-frequency energy. |
| Slow Sweep Pad | Slow evolving background chords and held notes. |

## Wanderer

Oscillator sync, sample-and-hold movement and ring modulation give distinct roles. Korg’s Odyssey patch book is a later manufacturer reference, not proof of an original hardware recording. Keep the sound-generating settings of Punch Bass and lower its final level to remove the measured output overshoot. [Reference](https://cdn.korg.com/us/support/download/files/fd6ff40cec0feaa6ef0df95b708e4202.pdf).

| Preset | Intended use |
|---|---|
| Init | General dual-oscillator starting point. |
| Sync Lead | Sweeping synchronized-oscillator lead. |
| S/H Bleeps | Discrete pitch/filter gestures for electronic punctuation. |
| Ring Bell | Inharmonic ringing line or tuned percussion. |
| Punch Bass | Short, forceful bass attacks with corrected final-output level. |
| PWM Lead | Moving pulse lead with a more regular harmonic body. |

## 8 Lire

Pair interaction, sharpness and delay feedback are central to the Lyra-inspired instrument. Reduce the extreme Feedback Howl settings while retaining it as the abrasive endpoint. A self-holding voice can keep sounding after note release. The SOMA manual describes behavior, not numerical parity for this implementation. [Reference](https://somasynths.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/LYRA-8_manual_Eng_V2_1.pdf).

| Preset | Intended use |
|---|---|
| Init | Open drone starting point for pair interaction. |
| Lyre Chord | Held chord with beating and gradual movement. |
| Eight Drone | Dense multi-voice drone bed. |
| Pair FM Bells | Interacting pairs for ringing, inharmonic tones. |
| Trill Delay | Pulsing repeated texture driven by delay motion. |
| Feedback Howl | Intentional abrasive transition, with reduced sharpness, delay modulation, drive and feedback. |

## Elvira 2

Digital voice forms, saturation, feedback and modulation allow both soft and abrasive drones. Deep Drone now uses a less aggressive voice/feedback combination. Pulse Lantern and Beating Cluster replace two overly forceful roles with pulsed and beating textures. Rusty Bells retains a clearly labelled abrasive role. [Reference](https://neutral-labs.com/elmyra2/downloads).

| Preset | Intended use |
|---|---|
| Init | General mixed-voice drone starting point. |
| Fifths Pad | Stable interval bed for slow harmonic changes. |
| Deep Drone | Low, less brittle sustained foundation. |
| Rusty Bells | Rough ringing accents; deliberate saturation and bright texture. |
| Pulse Lantern | Pulsed sustained texture with reduced bite and feedback. |
| Beating Cluster | Close-frequency beating with a restrained upper spectrum. |

## Scrounge

Nonlinear percussion is retained, with separate dry, dusty, broken and spacious roles. The original Scrooge manual informs the channel and drive concepts; preset authoring does not remove any implementation parity gap. [Reference](https://neutral-labs.com/scrooge).

| Preset | Intended use |
|---|---|
| Init | Balanced factory groove for the instrument’s default voices. |
| Dusty Boom Bap | Backbeat-led low/medium-energy groove. |
| Crunch Techno | Firm repeated low hits and driven percussion. |
| Broken Glitch | Displaced hits and fragmented noisy punctuation. |
| Slow Dub | Spaced low hits with room for tails. |
| Clean Electro | More regular, articulate electronic percussion. |

## Meridian 8

Keep nonlinear interaction audible while separating rhythmic density and pitch roles. Landscape’s NOON manual is the behavior reference. Tonal Percs supplies a pitched alternative to the rougher Industrial role. [Reference](https://www.landscape.fm/manuals).

| Preset | Intended use |
|---|---|
| Init | Baseline interacting-percussion groove. |
| Deep Techno | Low-centered regular pulse. |
| Broken Beat | Displaced accents and off-grid-feeling syncopation within the step grid. |
| Lo-fi Hip Hop | Sparse backbeat and quieter ghost hits. |

## Perun 4

The four voices cover both sharp attacks and sustained low tails. Keep Distorted EBM as a purposeful abrasive choice. The manufacturer’s Perkons manual explains the instrument reference; the 16-second audition does not establish indefinite feedback stability. [Reference](https://www.ericasynths.lv/media/PERKONS_manual_WEB_DEC_2024_Wa2wFLd.pdf).

| Preset | Intended use |
|---|---|
| Init | Baseline four-voice groove. |
| Techno Rumble | Long low-frequency body under a regular pulse. |
| Punchy Breaks | Separated kick/snare gestures with ghost hits. |
| Distorted EBM | Driven repetitive groove for aggressive bass-and-drum sections. |
| Dub Percussion | Sparse hits that expose the effect tail. |
| Clicky Minimal | Short high-definition transients with little overlap. |

## 909 Kit

A parent card exposes the existing kit controls without changing its child audio graph. Keep Init and add three distinct dance/percussion uses. The source is a current manufacturer description of the original machine; it is not an isolated original recording. [Reference](https://www.roland.com/global/products/rc_tr-909/).

| Preset | Intended use |
|---|---|
| Init | Original child sounds and baseline groove. |
| Warehouse | Driving dance pulse and open accents. |

| Dub Pressure | Spaced low hits and effect-tail exposure. |
