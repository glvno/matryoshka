# Device UI

IDs: D-200–D-249, D-900–D-949

The FM-1 is a patch player: patches are built in the browser, and the device plays them through one firmware build that runs one patch at a time. Every top-level module or assembly with card rows is a **device**, and Main, the Steps tracks and the Output are devices too, all in one **grouped order**. A device's card rows become **pages** of four, and K1..K4 are **edit bindings** to the page in focus. Navigation: on a device page HOME goes to the device's first page, then to the patch's home page ([D-243](device-ui.md#D-243)), and out of a dive (the menu, the environment, SEQ, SONG) it goes back along a **trail**; ALGO moves sideways, SELECT picks, SEL arms the device on screen ([D-235](device-ui.md#D-235)), REC records the armed tracks the same way on every page ([D-548](steps.md#D-548)), and PRESET changes patches, except on the **SEQ page**, where it steps the patch's pattern ([D-541](steps.md#D-541)). GLO, SEL and ENV are **fixed buttons** (GLO opens the FM-1's own settings page, ENV the **environment** page of devices and their presets), while FX, LFO, EDIT and ARP stay patch buttons, each with a **button kind**. The settings menu, the device page layout with its header, bars and footer, PRESET's toast in the footer, the LCD manual rules and the **panel core** complete the panel. While the editor's patch is over budget under Live, the device page shows the editor's page instead, and the FM-1 acts as that patch's controller. The FM-1 teaches its own controls: a **quick start** card after power-on, a **controls sheet** on OCT- + OCT+, a **nudge** when a control cannot act, and the **GUIDE** tab for the ideas behind them ([D-245](device-ui.md#D-245) to [D-249](device-ui.md#D-249)).
