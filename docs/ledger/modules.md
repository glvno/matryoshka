# Modules

IDs: D-400–D-449, D-750–D-799

Keys and MIDI reach a patch only through the **Main** module, whose `pitch` already carries pitch bend and whose `morph` drives **patch morphing** towards a **B side**. Most modules take two **parameter pins** with attenuverters, and a parameter's **dedicated input** is a permanent link, so a VCA with nothing at `cv` is closed. Every module except Main and Output can be bypassed and then passes its **through input**. Polyphony is built like a polysynth: Main's keys feed a **Voicing** module whose **poly assembly** runs once per voice, each part with its own **flaws**, and a Voicing can be limited to a channel and key range. The Arpeggiator, the **Drum map** and the Random module are ordinary patch modules. The [**Harmonizer**](vocabulary.md#harmonizer) plays a whole chord from each key, its [**chord buttons**](vocabulary.md#chord-button) picking the chord ([D-776](modules.md#D-776), [D-777](modules.md#D-777)). The Scope runs only in the editor, and shared effects run once per patch, as many as the FM-1's effect memory holds. A Delay's time plays up to its length, from 10 ms to 3.8 s, which its line in the FM-1's effect memory holds. Its RATE row trades sound quality for time: at 22k or 11k the same line holds two or four times as long, up to 15.2 s ([D-762](modules.md#D-762)). A new rate while it plays acts like a tape changing speed: the echoes in the line play on, lower or higher ([D-764](modules.md#D-764)). Its Tape switch adds a tape echo's darker repeats, saturation, wow and flutter, and up to three playback heads ([D-766](modules.md#D-766), [D-767](modules.md#D-767)). Module labels name the hardware they stand for and are the modules' LCD names, and type ids never change with a label. The Selector picks one of eight inputs or values with one stepped row and raises that position's gate ([D-427](modules.md#D-427)).
