# Steps

IDs: D-500–D-549, D-800–D-849

Steps is the sequencer for melodies, chords and drums: a **Steps track** holds sparse notes on a fixed step grid and plays its instrument through **keys out**, like Main's keys. In the editor a track is edited on its card's lanes or in the **piano roll** drawer; on the FM-1 it is edited on the **SEQ page**, XOX-style, on an 8 × 16 grid: a track that drives a **Drum map** shows its rows ([D-521](steps.md#D-521)), any other track a **piano roll** of 8 pitches ([D-527](steps.md#D-527)); whenever the keys reach a Drum map its rows are on black keys 1..8 ([D-520](steps.md#D-520)). **Arm** is a set: any device with a keys input can be armed, several at once, and while any is armed only armed devices hear Main's keys; with none armed the keys are [**wired**](vocabulary.md#wired). SEL arms on the FM-1, and the SEQ page arms the track it shows ([D-545](steps.md#D-545)). REC is separate from arming. In the editor, REC with the transport stopped is **cursor entry**, and running it records a **live take**. On the FM-1 REC is global ([D-548](steps.md#D-548)): on any page it starts the transport if it is stopped and records the armed Steps tracks over their notes, and PLAY stops both; with the menu's COUNT-IN setting on, it waits one or two bars first and records from a bar line ([D-803](steps.md#D-803)). The FM-1 arms and records from its own panel, the editor's FM-1 view mirrors its armed set, and the USB link does not carry the arm. A track holds up to 64 [**patterns**](vocabulary.md#pattern), saved in the patch ([D-539](steps.md#D-539)); the patch's one pattern number switches every track at the end of its pass ([D-540](steps.md#D-540)), and a [**song**](vocabulary.md#song) plays patterns in order with a shift each ([D-543](steps.md#D-543)).
