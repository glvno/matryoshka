# Steps

IDs: D-500–D-549

Steps is the sequencer for melodies, chords and drums: a **Steps track** holds sparse notes on a fixed step grid and plays its instrument through **keys out**, like Main's keys. In the editor a track is edited on its card's lanes or in the **piano roll** drawer; on the FM-1 it is edited on the **SEQ page**, XOX-style, and a track that drives a **Drum map** is edited by rows. **Arm** is a set: any device with a keys input can be armed, several at once, and while any is armed only armed devices hear Main's keys. REC is separate from arming: with the transport stopped it is **step entry**, and running it records a **live take**. The FM-1 arms and records from its own panel, the editor's FM-1 view mirrors its armed set, and the USB link does not carry the arm.
