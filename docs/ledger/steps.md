# Steps

IDs: D-500–D-549, D-800–D-849

Steps is the sequencer for melodies, chords and drums: a **Steps track** holds sparse notes on a fixed step grid and plays its instrument through **keys out**, like Main's keys. In the editor a track is edited on its card's lanes or in the **piano roll** drawer; on the FM-1 it is edited on the **SEQ page**, XOX-style, on an 8 × 16 grid: a track that drives a **Drum map** shows its rows ([D-521](steps.md#D-521)), any other track a **piano roll** of 8 pitches ([D-527](steps.md#D-527)); whenever the keys reach a Drum map its rows are on black keys 1..8 ([D-520](steps.md#D-520)). **Arm** is a set: any device with a keys input can be armed, several at once, and while any is armed only armed devices hear Main's keys; with none armed the keys are [**wired**](vocabulary.md#wired). SEL arms on the FM-1, and the SEQ page arms the track it shows ([D-545](steps.md#D-545)). REC is separate from arming. In the editor, REC with the transport stopped is **cursor entry**, and running it records a **live take**. On the FM-1's SEQ page, REC with the transport stopped starts [**step record**](vocabulary.md#step-record) on the first step of the page shown, and REC held + a white key starts it on that step: the keys write chords one step after another, and PLAY turns it into a live take ([D-804](steps.md#D-804)). The SEQ page's piano roll shows fixed pages of 8 pitches, and SELECT moves a page at a time ([D-809](steps.md#D-809)). On the FM-1's other pages REC is global ([D-548](steps.md#D-548)): it starts the transport if it is stopped and records the armed Steps tracks over their notes, and PLAY stops both; with the menu's COUNT-IN setting on, it waits one or two bars first and records from a bar line ([D-803](steps.md#D-803)). SEQ held + PLAY is [**play once**](vocabulary.md#play-once): a bar counted in with a click, then the pattern or the song once, and the transport stops by itself ([D-820](steps.md#D-820), proposed). The FM-1 arms and records from its own panel, and the editor's FM-1 view mirrors its armed set. Under Live the USB link carries the armed set both ways: the FM-1's to the editor ([D-802](steps.md#D-802)) and the editor's to firmware that takes it ([D-821](steps.md#D-821), proposed). A track holds up to 64 [**patterns**](vocabulary.md#pattern), saved in the patch ([D-539](steps.md#D-539)); the patch's one pattern number switches every track at the end of its pass ([D-540](steps.md#D-540)), and a [**song**](vocabulary.md#song) plays patterns in order with a shift each ([D-543](steps.md#D-543)). A step can hold [**P-locks**](vocabulary.md#p-lock): rows of the track's device set to a value of their own on that step only ([D-817](steps.md#D-817)).
