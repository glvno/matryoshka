# Patches and presets

IDs: D-300–D-349, D-850–D-899

Factory patches are the selected defaults, distinct from fitting candidates and archived Community data. The owner-approved 22-patch bank and 110 melodic sounds become production defaults without retuning ([D-898](#D-898)); final-version qualification and publication remain separate. New melodic revisions use an ordinary editable [**master FX bus**](vocabulary.md#master-fx-bus), omitting effects already on the instrument's interface ([D-884](#D-884)). Fitting compromises remain explicit ([D-885](#D-885), [D-889](#D-889), [D-891](#D-891)); Canvas and Expander's reduced-headroom exceptions remain narrowly scoped ([D-892](#D-892)). Once hardware polyphony parity is reached, full FX take priority over extra voices ([D-893](#D-893)); hardware-parity preset design uses Fmini-1 as a quality benchmark ([D-894](#D-894)). Editor examples remain available outside defaults, archived Community data retain attribution, and updates preserve saved slots ([D-890](#D-890), [D-886](#D-886), [D-887](#D-887)). A preset stores card values for one structure ([D-322](#D-322)); drum pattern ownership and complete audio/spectrogram evidence remain separate contracts ([D-882](#D-882), Proposed; [D-883](#D-883), Decided).
