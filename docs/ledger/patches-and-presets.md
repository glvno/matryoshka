# Patches and presets

IDs: D-300–D-349, D-850–D-899, D-1200–D-1249

Factory patches are the selected defaults, distinct from fitting candidates and archived Community data. The owner-approved 22-patch bank and 110 melodic sounds become production defaults without retuning ([D-898](#D-898)); Perun 4 and Spokes 6 left it for Community in 1.17, so it holds 20 ([D-1201](#D-1201)), Monolith D first ([D-1203](#D-1203)); Shush 101 joins it as a 21st slot ([D-1202](#D-1202)); final-version qualification and publication remain separate. Melodic instruments keep their added effects in an editable [**FX drawer**](vocabulary.md#fx-drawer), omitting effects already on the instrument's interface ([D-884](#D-884)), and their presets include the drawer ([D-1210](#D-1210)). Fitting compromises remain explicit ([D-885](#D-885), [D-889](#D-889), [D-891](#D-891)); Canvas and Expander's reduced-headroom exceptions remain narrowly scoped ([D-892](#D-892)). Capacity goes first to one voice's parity at up to four voices, then chorus, delay and reverb, then the original's voice count, then drive, or drive sooner when the next voice does not fit ([D-1200](#D-1200)); hardware-parity preset design uses Fmini-1 as a quality benchmark ([D-894](#D-894)). Editor examples remain available outside defaults, and archived Community data retain attribution ([D-890](#D-890), [D-886](#D-886)). Updates keep every saved slot a user changed, and give a slot that still holds a factory patch as a release wrote it today's patch of that name ([D-887](#D-887)). A preset stores card values for one structure ([D-322](#D-322)); drum pattern ownership and complete audio/spectrogram evidence remain separate contracts ([D-882](#D-882) and [D-883](#D-883), Decided). No factory patch or preset sounds until a key or PLAY: a drone plays from its PATTERN track, Canvas's pulser presets play from the keys, and a test holds every factory sound to it ([D-1205](#D-1205), [D-1206](#D-1206), Decided).
