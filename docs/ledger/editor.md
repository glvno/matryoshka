# Editor

IDs: D-550–D-649, D-950–D-999, D-1300–D-1349, D-1400–D-1449

The web editor is a canvas of cards: Main lives in the **Main dock** under it, and assemblies **expand** into frames and **collapse** back. Cards follow the "Edge" design: a choice is a **selector**, a cable dropped on a slider **thumb** routes to a parameter (there are no p1 / p2 jacks), and every edit is one undo step. A solid cable is one the user made, and a dashed **virtual cable** is something the app shows. The FM-1's limits appear as a **budget meter**, not as limits: a patch over them is a sandbox patch, shown in gold, that plays in the browser. One switch at the top, PATCH | EMU | LIBRARY, is the primary navigation: the canvas, the **Emulator view** (the real firmware UI built as WebAssembly, with the **controls strip** under it, [D-957](editor.md#D-957)) and the **Library** (what is stored, in the browser and on the FM-1, side by side; it opens nothing). The sidebar is the same in every view, and firmware installs open the **Install panel** over the main area. Patches stay in the browser and travel as **share links**, and only a link too long for a URL becomes a **stored link** on the site. Phones are not a target platform, but a patch can be opened, played and edited on one. Every output states its **output range** on hover ([D-646](editor.md#D-646)), and **comment boxes** of free text sit on the canvas ([D-950](editor.md#D-950)).

The instrument-interface design preserves modular layouts through optional assembly capabilities ([D-960](editor.md#D-960), Decided) and separates sound selection from performance settings ([D-961](editor.md#D-961), Decided). These are design constraints, not shipped capability controls.

The optional capability inspector and its bypass preview are experimental ([D-962](editor.md#D-962) and [D-963](editor.md#D-963), Decided).

Audio effects live on the canvas or in an instrument's [**FX drawer**](vocabulary.md#fx-drawer), whose chain the editor wires itself ([D-1337](editor.md#D-1337), [D-1346](editor.md#D-1346), Decided).
