# Engine

IDs: D-350–D-399, D-1100–D-1149

There are two audio engines: the TypeScript **reference engine** in the browser and its port to **fixed point** in the FM-1 firmware, kept in step by **golden kits** and shared parity rules, and a speed-up must be bit-exact, except for two rules both engines share. Both run at the FM-1's rate of 44118 Hz in 128-frame blocks. The FM-1 runs the **device form** of a patch, trimmed by a compile pass before encoding, skips **idle parts**, and sheds parts when the audio overloads. Poly assemblies sum their cards where cables leave them, and **paraphonic** ones mix each card at 1 / cards. The engine clock runs from Main's tempo unless MIDI clock drives it, and key events from Arpeggiators, Steps tracks and Drum maps land at the start of the next block. A patch that uses a [**browser-only ability**](vocabulary.md#browser-only-ability) counts as over the FM-1 budget, and so does one whose module lines do not fit the [**effect memory**](vocabulary.md#effect-memory). The editor shows a worst-case CPU estimate fitted to the 17 factory patches measured on the FM-1, and every module type needs a cost in that model.

Recognized master FX stages add bounded, runtime-only on/off behavior without changing saved parameters or generic bypass behavior ([D-1100](#D-1100)).
