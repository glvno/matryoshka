# The CPU estimate's voice slope and Expander S at eight voices, test rig, 2026-11-19

The CPU estimate ([D-365](../ledger/engine.md#D-365)) read Expander S at seven voices, with every stage of its master FX bus on, at 91.3 %, where the test rig held 81.6 % ([the 1.20 record](2026-11-18-rig-fxbus-voices.md)). This record finds the cause, refits the estimate on the 1.21 build, and raises Expander S to eight voices under [D-870](../ledger/patches-and-presets.md#D-870) and [D-1200](../ledger/patches-and-presets.md#D-1200) (#870).

## Build and method

- **Firmware:** main with #1102 (cheaper module copies) and #1105 (the block's fixed cost), CI run 44450005183. It was installed on the rig with `npm run device:scenarios -- --install 44450005183`, after the boot check (`emu:boot`, [D-123](../ledger/releases.md#D-123)). 55 of 58 scenarios passed, 3 skipped (the OUTPUT checks the rig's USB audio cannot hear), `checkDualCore` included.
- **Load** (`tools/rig_cpu_survey.py`): 0, 1, 4 and 8 held keys, 2 seconds each, transport stopped and running. Worst steady is the largest lower median of a phase's samples after the first, as a share of the 2,902 µs half-block. "Dropped" counts voices shed, overloads and late halves over all phases.
- **Every stage on:** each stage of the patch's master FX bus switched on with `setFxStageEnabled`, as `web/scripts/poly-bench.ts` does.
- **Block and module times:** `tools/m1_link.py --rig blocktime 3 --notes 8` and `modtime 3 --notes 8`.
- **Emulator:** `m1emu cpu --modtime` under the timing model (`M1EMU_TIMING=tools/emu/timing.txt`, [docs/EMULATOR.md](../EMULATOR.md#the-timing-model)) on the same build.

## The cause

Expander S with every stage on, worst steady %, stopped / running on the rig; the emulator's mean audio interrupt at 8 keys; the estimate on main's table and after the refit:

| Voices | Rig | Emulator | Estimate, main | Estimate, refit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 50.5 / 51.4 | 50.3 | 52.9 | 54.7 |
| 4 | 55.6 / 56.7 | 56.3 | 60.3 | 60.1 |
| 5 | 63.0 / 65.0 | 64.5 | 70.3 | 67.5 |
| 6 | 68.8 / 71.1 | 70.6 | 77.6 | 72.8 |
| 7 | 76.4 / 77.2 | 78.5 | 87.3 | 80.0 |
| 8 | 81.0 / 82.7 | 84.2 | 94.6 | 85.9 |

- **The slope.** On the rig each voice from four to eight adds 6.3 points; in the emulator 7.0. The estimate on main's table added 8.6 points a voice. The intercept was right and the slope was too steep.
- **The two cores share each voice evenly.** At 8 keys, four to eight voices, the audio interrupt grows from 1,547 to 2,317 µs. Core 0's time in the voices grows by 769 µs and core 1's by 762 µs. Each added voice costs each core about 190 µs.
- **Per module** (module times at 4 voices): the voices took 1,694 µs on the rig, against 2,063 µs at the table's scale with no factor. The modules outside the voices took 925 µs. The estimate gave them 566 µs: its master FX bus counts at 0.53, the factor fitted to the rig's rise when the stages are switched on, and its Reverb, Chorus and Delay read 0.57 to 0.69 of their rig time.
- **Why that steepens the slope.** The estimate credits the second core with 0.75 of its modelled saving, so each added voice counts on core 0 at 0.625 of its cycles. The fit chose the pair of an engine factor and a voice factor that read at least the rig at every count measured, with the engine factor capped at 1.5. Expander S's modules outside its voices are few, so its engine factor could not make up the shortfall at three and four voices. The voice factor had to, which made it 0.76 where the rig's rises need 0.58.
- **What did not fix it** (refits on the 1.20 surveys): the second core's credit at 1.0 or 1.2 (Expander S at seven with every stage on 93.0 % and 94.1 %), the FX bus factor at 0.85 (94.9 %), and the engine factor's cap at 4 (88.9 %).

## The refit

`web/scripts/cpu-rig-fit.ts` (frozen in `web/scripts/cpu-costs.ts` `RIG`):

- An engine the rig measured at several voice counts takes its factor from the rig's rise from one count to the next: least squares over every patch of that engine measured at several counts, as shipped and with every stage on. The same factor counts for its other modules.
- Such an engine also gets its own time a block (`blockUs`), the least that makes the estimate read at least the rig at every count. Oracle V 260 µs, Cassia 1 256, Expander S 249, Hera 221, Io 4 424, Algo Four 85.
- The targets are the 1.14 bank and its poly patches at more voices, today's 21 factory patches with every stage on, and today's poly patches at several counts, all on this build.
- Every target reads at least the rig. The poly patches read 0 to 3.4 points over it, the 1.14 bank 0 to 5.4 (Scrounge), and today's bank 0.1 to 6.4 (808 Kit). Main's table read several of today's patches under the rig with the transport running: 909 Kit 64.8 % against 68.9 %, Scrounge 71.1 % against 75.5 %, Elvira 2 75.3 % against 76.8 %, Shush 101 43.8 % against 45.3 %, Io 4 at two voices with every stage on 46.5 % against 49.9 %.

## Voices

- **Expander S plays eight voices**, the Oberheim Eight Voice's count. With every stage on, the estimate reads 85.9 % and the rig held 81.0 % stopped and 82.7 % running. Its VOICES row has no top.
- Expander S's factory presets with every stage on, at the voices each plays: Init 81.6 / 82.6, SEM Brass 83.0 / 84.3, Notch Strings 81.2 / 82.2, Highpass Pluck 81.2 / 82.0, Velvet Bloom 77.8 / 78.3, Dusk Chord 74.4 / 75.7, Unison Lead (four voices) 57.1 / 59.8, SEM Bass (one voice) 40.6 / 42.0. Nothing dropped. The estimates are 85.9, 85.3, 85.0, 85.9, 83.2, 83.2, 59.0 and 41.4 %.
- Notch Strings plays the patch's eight, where it set six. Unison Lead sets four voices: four on one key, as the Four Voice's unison. SEM Bass keeps its one.
- **No other patch rises.** Oracle V at six needs 377,016 bytes of patch memory, over the 370,824 the FM-1 has. Cassia 1 at six needs 257 module copies, over 254, and more memory too. Oracle V (five, the Prophet-5), Hera 6 (six, the Juno-106) and Io 4 (four, the Jupiter-4) play their original's count. Algo Four plays eight.

## Headroom and loudness

At eight keys, Expander S's Highpass Pluck peaked 1.18 at its level 1.58, and its chord is the loudness rule's bound. Its level row is 0.92 (gain only): eight keys peak 0.69 and it lands on the rule. Dusk Chord's level row stays at its top (2), now 2.9 dB under the rule (`web/src/levels.ts` `SHORT`). Every other Expander S sound peaks 0.86 or under.
