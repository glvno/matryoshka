# Factory voices on the 1.20 build, test rig, 2026-11-18

The VOICES tops of [D-870](../ledger/patches-and-presets.md#D-870) worked out again on the 1.20 engine and the CPU estimate refitted to it ([the 1.20 headroom record](2026-11-18-headroom-120.md)), then checked on the test rig at the new counts (#782).

## Build and method

- **Firmware:** CI run 44313018225 (branch `voices-1.20`), installed on the rig through the boot check (`emu:boot`, [D-123](../ledger/releases.md#D-123)); `checkDualCore` passed on it (22 of 22 checks).
- **Patches:** each poly factory patch at the voices shown, as shipped and with every stage of its master FX bus on (`fx`).
- **Load** (`tools/rig_cpu_survey.py`): 0, 1, 4 and 8 held keys, 2 seconds each, transport stopped and running. Worst steady is the largest lower median of a phase's samples after the first, as a share of the 2,902 µs half-block. "Dropped" counts voices shed, overloads and late halves over all phases.
