# PR #775 on the test rig, 2026-11-08

The build of PR #775 (`fit-1.11` at c3681db4, firmware CI run 42529568698) was surveyed on the test rig before merge, as [D-141](../ledger/releases.md#D-141) asks for a voice-count change. The main session ran the survey; this pull request did not touch an FM-1. The patches are this build's factory set: the CPU model refitted on the rig ([D-365](../ledger/engine.md#D-365)), Oracle V at four voices, Cassia 1 at two, Hera 4, and the 808 Kit and Verona Drum 1 in their 1.10 layout ([D-879](../ledger/patches-and-presets.md#D-879), [D-880](../ledger/patches-and-presets.md#D-880)).

Measured with `tools/rig_cpu_survey.py`, as in [the 1.11 batch](2026-11-08-rig-release-1.11.md): each patch uploaded live, then held at rest and with 1, 4 and 8 keys over USB MIDI. Files:

- `cpu-survey-2026-11-08-rig-pr775.json`: every factory patch, the transport stopped.
- `cpu-survey-2026-11-08-rig-pr775-run.json`: every factory patch, the transport running.

Every patch plays with nothing shed and no overload, but one. Canvas with eight keys held and the transport running was muted by the overload guard. Stopped, its guard mean with eight keys was 2,565 µs (88 %), close to the guard's 90 % limit. The next closest are Briefcase 3 (2,557 µs mean with eight keys), Verona Drum 1 (91 % steady peak) and the 808 Kit (87 %); all of them played clean.

After this survey Canvas lost its PATTERN track and keeps the Arpeggiator alone (`web/scripts/factory-build.ts` players), as Io 4 and Algo Four do. In the model PATTERN cost Canvas about 2.7 points. The rig re-surveys Canvas on the merged head.
